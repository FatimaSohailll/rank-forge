How it works

  1. Keyword ScoutScores ideas by demand, buyer intent, competition and commission.
  2. Content PlannerReads the live top results and writes a brief with a better angle.
  3. WriterDrafts the full article in your saved tone and niche.
  4. Fact Checker ‖ Link BuilderRun in parallel: claims are checked on the web, affiliate links placed by code.
  5. EditorFixes flagged claims, audits SEO and improves the score.
  6. You approveNothing goes live until you say so. Approval can't be faked by the model.
  7. Rank + Click Trackers → OptimizerWatches positions and affiliate clicks, then recommends a rewrite or a new target.

Safe by design

Honest numbers
